HOW TO PLAY

Survive enemy waves. A mothership appears every 5 waves. Chain kills to multiply points. Earn credits and unlock ships in the hangar.

DESKTOP

WASD or arrow keys move your ship.
SPACE shoots (or toggle auto-fire with F). ESC pauses.

MOBILE

Drag your finger across the game area to move.
Shooting is automatic. Use the ❚❚ button to pause.

ENEMIES

  • Scout — flies straight down.
  • Serpent — moves in a zigzag.
  • Gunner — aims and fires at you.

TEMPORARY POWER-UPS

  • SShield — absorbs one hit.
  • 2Double shot — fires two projectiles.
  • RRapid fire — increases firing speed.

BOSSES

The mothership alternates between spread shots and radial bursts. Stay away from the center and dodge through gaps.
